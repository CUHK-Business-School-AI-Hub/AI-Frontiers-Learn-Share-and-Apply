About the session

AI has made review almost effortless. An agent can check a syllabus, slides or an examination paper in seconds, and our students now do the same with their own work, and with ours. This session discusses how AI can support our teaching in two ways: helping us review the materials we teach with, and helping our students learn to judge what AI tells them. We will walk through how to work with agents to get genuinely useful feedback, what AI tends to notice in teaching materials and what it tends to miss, and how we might teach when our students have the same tools. A live demo takes a real lecture deck through review and revamp, from what the agent finds to what we decide to change. Along the way, we mark where our own judgement has to come in.

Open to all faculty members and RPg students. Administrative staff are also welcome.
