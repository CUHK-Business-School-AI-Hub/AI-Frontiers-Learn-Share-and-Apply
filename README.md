# AI Frontiers: Learn, Share, Apply

**AI Frontiers: Learn, Share, Apply** is the AI Hub’s monthly training and knowledge-sharing series for faculty and staff. The series brings together colleagues from across the Faculty and beyond to explore how emerging AI technologies can be understood, evaluated and applied in our research, teaching and everyday work.

Through practical demonstrations, experience-sharing, hands-on training and focused discussions, each session will introduce useful tools, proven workflows and important developments in AI. Topics will include:

- HPC cluster resources and how to use them
- Open-source tools for AI research and development
- Toolkits and platforms developed by the AI Hub
- AI agents and agent-based workflows
- Responsible AI, data considerations and AI governance
- Practical applications of AI in research and teaching
- Emerging technologies and other topics of common interest

The series welcomes participants at every level of technical experience. Whether you are beginning to explore AI or already applying it in your work, AI Frontiers offers an opportunity to build practical skills, exchange ideas and connect with colleagues who share similar interests.

We also welcome contributions from across our community. If you would like to lead or contribute to a session, share a useful tool or case study, or suggest a topic that you would like the series to cover, please contact Prof. [Philip Zhang](https://rphilipzhang.github.io/rphilipzhang/), Co-director of AI Hub for Business, at [philipzhang@cuhk.edu.hk](mailto:philipzhang@cuhk.edu.hk).
