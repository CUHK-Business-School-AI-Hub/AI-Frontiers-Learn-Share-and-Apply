# AI Frontiers: Learn, Share, Apply

Welcome to **AI Frontiers**, the AI Hub’s monthly training and knowledge-sharing series for faculty and staff at CUHK Business School, with colleagues from across the Faculty and beyond welcome to join.

AI is moving quickly, and it can be hard to know where to start or what is worth trying. This series gives us a chance to explore it together: try useful tools, learn from each other’s experience, and find ways to put AI to work in research, teaching and everyday tasks.

## What to expect

Sessions bring together practical demonstrations, hands-on training, experience-sharing and time for discussion. The aim is to help you understand what a tool can do, judge whether it fits your needs, and see how you might use it in your own work.

Topics will include:

- **AI in research and teaching** — practical examples and approaches you can learn from.
- **AI agents and workflows** — how agents work and where they might help with your tasks.
- **Tools to try** — open-source tools for AI research and development, alongside toolkits and platforms built by the AI Hub.
- **Computing resources** — how to get started with high-performance computing (HPC) clusters and use the resources available.
- **Responsible use of AI** — thinking through data, responsible AI and governance questions.
- **New developments** — emerging technologies and other topics our community wants to explore.

## Come as you are

You are welcome whether you are just getting curious about AI or already using it regularly. Bring your questions, something you would like to try, or an experience others could learn from. There is room to learn at every level, and you do not need to have all the answers to take part.

## Have something to share?

Found a useful tool? Tried an approach that worked well—or one that taught you what to avoid? We would love to hear about it.

You can lead or contribute to a session, share a case study, or suggest a topic you would like us to cover. To get involved, contact **Prof. [Philip Zhang](https://rphilipzhang.github.io/rphilipzhang/)**, Co-director of AI Hub for Business, at [philipzhang@cuhk.edu.hk](mailto:philipzhang@cuhk.edu.hk).
