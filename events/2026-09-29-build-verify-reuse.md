## Abstract

Generative AI gives us a new way to put computing power to work: natural language. But how do we turn that capability into better teaching, research, and administration? This interactive 90-minute session shows our faculty, PhD students, and staff how to redesign everyday workflows around AI, achieving substantial boosts in productivity and quality. Through practical demonstrations, we will move from a real task to a useful output, learn to check results and diagnose failures, and turn successful approaches into reusable procedures. We will also explore how context compounds: organizing trusted information, proven methods, and professional judgment so that each new task builds on previous learning. Participants will leave with a concrete workflow to improve and a simple plan to assess gains in both productivity and quality. No coding background is required.
