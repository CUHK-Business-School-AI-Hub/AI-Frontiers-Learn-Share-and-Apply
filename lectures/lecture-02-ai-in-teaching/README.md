# Lecture 02 · Using AI in Teaching

**Reviewing Materials and Guiding Students** · Prof. Qi Zhang · 27 October 2026

Part of AI Frontiers, CUHK Business School's internal training series.

The session explores how AI can help review teaching materials and how to guide students in judging AI feedback. A live demonstration takes a real lecture deck through review and revision, highlighting where the teacher's judgement is needed.
