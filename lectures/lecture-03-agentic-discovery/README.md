# Lecture 03 · From Agent Harness to Agentic Discovery

**External Speaker Seminar #1** · Dr. Bolin DING · 10 November 2026

**Full title:** From Agent Harness to Agentic Discovery: Building LLM Agents with AgentScope and Scaling Scientific Reasoning with InfinityScope

Part of AI Frontiers: Learn, Share, Apply. Hosted by Tony Song AI Hub for Business, CUHK Business School, The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

The session covers the progression from LLM-powered data operations to agents for scientific research, the AgentScope framework for building, deploying and tuning agents, and InfinityScope, an agentic workspace in which scientists guide teams of agents through reasoning and verification.

## Event details

- **Date and time:** 10 November 2026 (Tuesday), 2:30–4:00 pm (Hong Kong time).
- **Venue:** CYT 928, Cheng Yu Tung Building, CUHK.
- **Speaker:** Dr. Bolin DING, Senior Director of Research, Alibaba Token Hub.
- **Audience:** All faculty members and RPg students.
